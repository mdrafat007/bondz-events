# Bondz Events synchronization plan

## Outcome
A single six-step booking experience powers both `/book` and the animated home showcase. Marketing pages, navigation, footer, copy, and responsive presentation follow the supplied Bondz specification while the existing Lovable preview and TanStack routes remain intact.

## What will change
1. **Unify the booking engine.** Keep `BookingEngine` with its existing `BookingProvider` and `useBookingState` as the only source of booking screens and state. Remove the home showcase's parallel step components and hand-built state. Introduce a display-only demo mode for the same engine, with isolated sample data, no payment, signature submission, navigation side effects, downloads, or misleading real dispatch. Keep `/book` fully interactive and the separate preview routes untouched.
2. **Make the showcase a true timed demonstration.** Drive the real engine through six steps at 3.2 seconds each, with roughly one-second visible actions: wedding and category-matched vibes, location and guests 45-90, selected services and available partners, a genuinely open date with Evening slot, sample details and mock signature, then example confirmation and dispatch. Scroll the engine's actual canvas to relevant content, loop cleanly, pause on hover/focus, and launch the booking curtain on click or keyboard activation. Keep only the bottom Live Demo caption and an elapsed-time scrubber. Invert its theme using project-level Bondz tokens rather than hardcoded colors.
3. **Align booking rules and completion.** Keep the eight events, tailored vibe choices, 75-day deterministic calendar, and 25% deposit. Align the service presentation with the requested cocktail while retaining compatible vendor categories; offer Morning, Afternoon, Evening consistently wherever slots appear. Prevent selection of unavailable date/slot combinations and reset invalid selections when inputs change. Change all balance messaging to 14 days before the event, and add a downloadable `.ics`, the existing `/invite/$ref` link, a restart action, and a clearly labeled simulated host/venue/partner/Mr. Bondz dispatch checklist. Preserve the eight-clause agreement; show the six cancellation/rescheduling policy terms separately in the footer.
4. **Recompose the home screen.** Fit the specified headline into exactly two lines at tested widths without clipping, retain the mascot CTA, place all four pillars directly beneath it, and give the marquee area the requested clearance. Keep the two existing testimonial/partner tracks with portraits and availability indicators. Ensure the live engine is legible inside the 50/50 desktop showcase and stacked mobile layout.
5. **Synchronize the site chrome and editorial pages.** Preserve the seven numbered navigation destinations, booking takeover, standalone component/mockup previews, tactile preference controls and mobile menu. Make the footer policy keyboard-accessible and responsive. Review and align the existing `/how-it-works`, `/services`, `/partners`, `/portfolios`, `/contact`, `/connect-ai`, and `/invite/$ref` content with the named sections and working interactions; retain their distinct metadata. Replace em/en dashes in app-owned displayed copy and metadata only, without editing managed library files or generated output.
6. **Validate the whole journey.** Check home and all content pages at narrow mobile, tablet, and desktop widths, both themes, reduced motion, and keyboard access. Confirm timed demo transitions, pause/resume, click-through, real booking date constraints, 25%/75% breakdown, sample-free actual booking confirmation, calendar download, invite destination, and no sideways scrolling or preview errors.

## Technical boundaries
- The attached design-system library stays untouched. Compose its exported controls and assets, with project-layer token overrides only for the explicitly requested palette and any missing semantic roles. The requested exact palette and two-line typography are intentional deviations from current library values.
- The project has a deterministic client-side demo, not connected calendars, real payments, or actual notification delivery. On-screen language must not claim those operations happened. No backend or payment integration is added in this work.
- Reuse TanStack Start's file routes, shell, and existing curtain rather than replacing the router. Avoid broad deletion of unused files until references are checked.
